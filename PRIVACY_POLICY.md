# Privacy Policy for Dabbu

**Last Updated:** 14th January 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Dabbu** . We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our mobile application (.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information about you when you use our App. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 SMS Data (Critical Permission)
Dabbu requests permission to **read and receive SMS messages** on your device.
*   **Purpose:** The core functionality of Dabbu is to automatically track your expenses by parsing transaction-related SMS messages (e.g., from banks, credit cards, UPI).
*   **Processing:** All SMS parsing and data extraction occur **locally on your device**.
*   **Privacy:** We **DO NOT** upload your raw SMS messages or financial data to any external server. Your personal financial data remains strictly on your device.

### 2.2 Local Storage
We store the extracted transaction data, your budget goals, and preferences locally on your device using secure local database technologies.

### 2.3 Device Information
We may collect minimal device-specific information (such as operating system version) solely for the purpose of app functionality and debugging local issues.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:
*   Categorize and display your expenses.
*   Generate financial insights and charts.
*   Send you local notifications regarding your spending limits or goals.
*   Maintain and improve the App's performance.

## 4. Permissions We Request

To provide our services, we request the following permissions:
*   **READ_SMS & RECEIVE_SMS:** To detect and parse financial transactions.
*   **POST_NOTIFICATIONS:** To send you alerts about your budgets and goals.
*   **SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM:** To schedule timely background tasks or reminders.
*   **IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS:** To ensure the background service can verify transactions without interruption.

## 5. Data Sharing and Third Parties
*   **No Cloud Sync:** Currently, Dabbu operates as an offline-first application. We do not share your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.
*   **Service Providers:** We may use standard platform services (like Google Play Services) which may collect anonymous usage data for crash reporting or app delivery.

## 6. Data Retention
Your data is retained on your device for as long as you use the App. You can clear your data at any time by uninstalling the App or using the "Clear Data" option within the App settings.

## 7. Security
We take reasonable measures to help protect information about you from loss, theft, misuse, and unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 8. Children's Privacy
Our App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13.
